Privacy field note · 28 August 2026

Privacy is the default route.

Client, quote and change data stays in your browser's IndexedDB. Change Ledger has no account system, analytics, advertising pixels or cloud database.

What is stored

The app stores the clients, projects, base quotes, change cards, frozen approval snapshots and imported receipts you enter. A purchase license and its cached verification result are stored separately in localStorage. Removing site data in your browser removes this information, so download backups regularly.

Approval and return links

An approval link contains the selected project summary, client name, change terms, issue time and content hash in the URL fragment (the portion after #). Browsers do not send URL fragments to the hosting server. Anyone who receives the full link can read that frozen record, so share it only with the intended client. Return links similarly contain the decision receipt. No email address is included in an approval link.

Exports

JSON backups, CSV files, printed PDFs and receipt files are created on your device. You choose where they go and who receives them. We cannot recover deleted browser data.

License verification

If you add a paid license, the app sends only that token to the Sociobot verification endpoint on first unlock and at most once per day. Sociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record for checkout. Its checkout privacy terms apply there. No project or client content is included in verification.

Network and service worker

The app shell is cached locally for offline use. There are no third-party fonts, scripts or runtime CDNs. Ordinary web-server security logs may contain an IP address and requested path, but not URL-fragment approval contents.

Your controls

Use “Import or back up data” to export or restore the workspace. Use your browser's site-data controls to erase it. Questions can be sent through the contact channel on sociobot.in.
